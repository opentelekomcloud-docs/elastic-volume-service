Disabling the Recycle Bin
=========================

Scenarios
---------

You can disable the recycle bin if you no longer need it.

Constraints
-----------

You must empty the recycle bin before disabling it. To empty the recycle bin, you can:

-  Recover the disks from the recycle bin by referring to :ref:`Recovering Disks from the Recycle Bin <evs_01_0067>`.
-  Permanently delete the disks from the recycle bin by referring to :ref:`Permanently Deleting Disks from the Recycle Bin <evs_01_0068>`.

Procedure
---------

#. Sign in to the console.

#. Click |image1| in the upper left corner and select a region.

#. Click |image2| in the upper left corner and choose **Storage** > **Elastic Volume Service**.

   The **Elastic Volume Service** page is displayed.

#. Click the **Recycle Bin** tab.

#. In the upper right corner of the **Recycle Bin** tab page, click **Disable Recycle Bin**.

   A dialog box is displayed.

#. Click **OK**.

   When message "Recycle bin is disabled" is displayed, the recycle bin is disabled successfully.
